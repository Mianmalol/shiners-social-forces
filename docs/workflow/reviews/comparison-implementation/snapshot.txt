## Conventions

- **Units are video pixels.** The image is 1920 × 1032 px (1890 × 1080 for the 150-fish
  trial), and **y points down**. A fish's right side is the heading rotated by +90°
  in these coordinates. No pixel-to-centimetre scale is recorded in the source data;
  `body_length` (about 38 px) is the natural length unit.
- **The heading is measured**, from the tracker's body-axis fit, not derived from the
  direction of travel. It differs from the direction of travel by a median of 3.5°.
- **The midline** consists of 21 points at equal arc spacing along the body. Its total
  length equals `body_length`. Checked against the video, the midline points lie a

- **`body_length` is half the tracker's raw value.** The tracker's value overshoots the
  visible fish about two-fold. In the nine 10-, 30- and 70-fish trials the halved value
  matches the visible fish in the video. The same factor of 0.5 is used for the

## Time and gaps

Each trial was recorded as seven consecutive segments of about 2 minutes. The tracker
skipped one video frame per segment, so **one frame is missing at every segment join**.
The trajectories confirm this: extrapolating each fish's velocity across a join gives
about 2 frames elapsed. `frame` counts video frames, so at a join it jumps by 2 and
`segment` increments.

The positions next to a join are also noisier. The first tracked frame of a segment
deviates about 1.5 px from a smooth path, against about 0.5 px elsewhere, and the last
frame before a join about 0.8 px. A velocity computed with the correct time step is
therefore still about 1.2 times too high across a join, and an acceleration shows a
spike there.

**For anything that takes time derivatives, treat each segment as its own continuous
recording.** Do not difference across a join:

for (fish, seg), track in df.groupby(["id", "segment"]):

| --- | --- |

sees `id2` when at least one ray from either eye hits `id2` first. Each eye covers its
own side plus 12.5 degrees across the midline, which leaves a 25-degree blind wedge
behind the fish. Fish of the same group block each other's view; there is no partial
transparency.

## Material findings

### M1. Excluding whole frames for one fish's heading jump biases P by N
`docs/methods.md` (B1/B3) drops a whole frame from polarization if any fish's SG support touches a heading jump. If the per-fish-frame exclusion probability is *p*, frame retention is about (1−p)^N:

| p | N=10 | N=151 |
|---|---|---|
| 0.001 | 0.99 | 0.86 |
| 0.01 | 0.90 | 0.22 |

Excluded frames are probably not random. They are likely enriched for fast turns, crowding and tracking stress. So P at N=151 (and to a lesser degree N=70) would describe a progressively more selected subset of time.

**Fix:**
- Report the frame-retention fraction for P per trial, broken down by exclusion reason.
- Add a sensitivity analysis that drops only the affected fish rather than the whole frame. Record n per frame, and compare medians on the common frame set.
- If the cross-N ordering of P differs between the two masks, report the pattern as unresolved.

### M2. P has no finite-n reference saved, unlike C
For iid uniform headings, E[P²] = 1/n exactly and E[P] ≈ √(π/(4n)). That gives roughly 0.28, 0.16, 0.11 and 0.072 for n = 10, 30, 70 and 151. A raw decline of P with N is therefore expected even with no change in behavior.

**Fix:**
- Save √(π/(4n)) per frame next to P, with the same "illustrative, not a null" label used for C.
- State in the narrative that any P-vs-N statement must be read against this reference.
- Do not subtract or divide by it, matching the C rule.

### M3. The N=151 length scenario covers too few outputs
Every output scaled by L shifts under L×1.15:

- **Simple rescales:** spacing and spread divide by 1.15; body-frame density per L² multiplies by 1.15² ≈ 1.32; centroid speed and k·L also shift.
- **Outputs that need recomputation:** anything with a speed-dependent cutoff, i.e. slip eligibility, C/P eligibility, and the threshold sweep.
- **Not fixable by rescaling:**
  - **Curvature.** The source says the midline is about 15% short yet still fits the shape. That could be uniform scaling or truncation (for example, a missing tail tip). If it is truncation, midline indices 7..13 are not the anatomical midpoint, so k is not comparable to the other trials.
  - **Visual network.** It was ray-cast from the stored, too-short outlines. That plausibly lowers occlusion, raises degree and visible fraction, and shrinks angular sizes.

**Fix:**
- Apply the L×1.15 scenario to all L-normalized outputs, recomputing those with eligibility cutoffs.
- Mark N=151 curvature and network metrics as length-biased in an unknown direction or size, with no available correction.
- Exclude N=151 from any claim that curvature or visibility varies with N.

### M4. Other N=151 confounds need a statement
The N=151 trial differs in more than length:

- **Duration:** 17,999 frames (about 10 min) vs about 24,580 (about 13.7 min). If behavior drifts within a trial (acclimation), comparing the full duration mixes in a time-window effect.
- **Time base:** `time = frame/29.97` is a converter convention. The raw video name `calibrated_lowres30.25.mp4` does not confirm the frame rate, and the frame rate cannot be checked from the tracks. Speeds, turn rates and the BL/s thresholds all depend on it.
- **Segment structure:** this is a single unjoined video, so the seven-segment join structure may not apply. The plan already says to use actual segments.
- **Chirality:** a different camera or calibration could flip the image.

**Fix:**
- Recompute the smaller trials' summaries on their first 17,999 frames as a matched-duration sensitivity.
- Record the 29.97 fps assumption for N=151 as unverified.
- Do not compare signed C (direction of rotation) across cameras.

### M5. Threshold-intersection comparison: say what it actually isolates
Frame eligibility is monotone in the threshold. Reaching ≥80% of fish at 1.0 BL/s implies reaching it at 0.5 and 0.25. So the "intersection of eligible frames" is exactly the 1.0-eligible frame set, a strongly selected high-relative-motion subset. Holding fish fixed as well makes C identical across thresholds, which is trivial.

**Fix:** report two explicit components:
1. **Frame-selection effect:** C at a single threshold, computed on each threshold's frame set.
2. **Fish-composition effect:** different thresholds compared on the common (1.0-eligible) frame set.

Label the common set as "high relative motion frames", not as representative.

### M6. Pre-specify what counts as a cross-trial pattern
The plan bans significance tests but does not define "consistent across trials". That leaves room for post hoc reading of 3+3+3+1 points.

**Fix:** before results are viewed, fix a descriptive criterion. For example: "monotone in N" means every trial at a smaller N lies on the same side of every trial at the next N, so the ranges do not overlap. Anything else is reported as overlapping or inconsistent. N=151 can be at most "consistent with" the trend and never establishes it.

def file_hash(path):
    h = hashlib.sha256()

def validate_tracks(tracks):
    """Validate the projected fields actually used; do not claim unselected QC."""
    required = BASE_COLUMNS + IDENTITY_COLUMNS + POSE_COLUMNS
    missing = sorted(set(required)-set(tracks.columns))

def common_cohort_summaries(tables, labels, label_name, require_polarization=True):

"""Descriptive pilot measurements defined in docs/methods.md.

Inputs are one trial. Pixels use image coordinates (y down). Undefined quantities
remain NaN; functions do not impute missing samples or infer behavioral responses.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from scipy.signal import savgol_filter


def _single_trial(tracks):
    for column in ("group", "sequence"):
        if column in tracks and tracks[column].nunique(dropna=False) > 1:
            raise ValueError("Metrics require one trial at a time")
    if tracks.empty:
        raise ValueError("Empty tracks")
    if tracks.duplicated(["frame", "id"]).any():
        raise ValueError("Duplicate track keys")


def _scale(length_scale):
    if not np.isfinite(length_scale) or length_scale <= 0:
        raise ValueError("Length scale must be finite and positive")


def _dense(tracks, columns):
    """A frame-by-fish view with explicit identical fish coverage."""
    _single_trial(tracks)
    frames = np.sort(tracks.frame.unique())
    fish = np.sort(tracks.id.unique())
    ordered = tracks.sort_values(["frame", "id"])
    if len(ordered) != len(frames) * len(fish):
        raise ValueError("Incomplete fish set")
    # Unique keys plus the full Cartesian cardinality ensures every fish/frame.
    arrays = {column: ordered[column].to_numpy(dtype=float).reshape(len(frames), len(fish))
              for column in columns}
    return frames, fish, ordered, arrays


def kinematics(tracks, window=11, order=3):
    """Return original rows/index plus segment- and gap-safe temporal derivatives.

    vx/vy: pixels/s; ax/ay: pixels/s²; turn_rate: radians/s (clockwise positive).
    Discard half a smoothing window plus one sample at both block ends, so the
    known noisier first/last segment samples do not enter retained estimates.
    """
    _single_trial(tracks)
    if not isinstance(window, (int, np.integer)) or window < 3 or window % 2 != 1:
        raise ValueError("Window must be an odd integer >= 3")
    if not isinstance(order, (int, np.integer)) or order < 2 or order >= window:
        raise ValueError("Polynomial order must be >= 2 and below window")
    if not np.isfinite(tracks[["time", "frame", "X", "Y", "ANGLE"]].to_numpy(dtype=float)).all():
        raise ValueError("Nonfinite kinematics input")
    result = tracks.copy()
    output = {name: np.full(len(tracks), np.nan) for name in ["xs", "ys", "vx", "vy", "ax", "ay", "turn_rate"]}
    jump = np.zeros(len(tracks), dtype=bool)
    heading_valid = np.ones(len(tracks), dtype=bool)
    half = window // 2
    trim = half + 1
    # groupby.indices uses positional indexes, preserving even nonunique row labels.
    for positions in tracks.groupby(["id", "segment"], sort=False).indices.values():
        positions = positions[np.argsort(tracks.frame.to_numpy()[positions], kind="stable")]
        frame = tracks.frame.to_numpy()[positions]
        for block in np.split(positions, np.flatnonzero(np.diff(frame) != 1) + 1):
            if len(block) < 2:
                continue
            sample = tracks.iloc[block]
            heading = sample.ANGLE.to_numpy(dtype=float)
            step = (np.diff(heading) + np.pi) % (2*np.pi) - np.pi
            flagged = np.flatnonzero(np.abs(step) > np.pi/2)
            block_jump = np.zeros(len(block), dtype=bool)
            block_jump[flagged] = True
            block_jump[flagged+1] = True
            jump[block] = block_jump
            contaminated = np.convolve(np.pad(block_jump, (half, half)), np.ones(window), mode="valid") > 0
            heading_valid[block] = ~contaminated
            delta = np.diff(sample.time.to_numpy(dtype=float))
            dt = float(np.median(delta))
            if dt <= 0 or not np.allclose(delta, dt, rtol=1e-6, atol=1e-10):
                raise ValueError("Irregular or nonpositive time increments within a continuous block")
            if len(block) <= 2*trim:
                continue
            interior = block[trim:-trim]
            for coordinate, velocity, acceleration in [("X", "vx", "ax"), ("Y", "vy", "ay")]:
                values = sample[coordinate].to_numpy(dtype=float)
                smooth = savgol_filter(values, window, order, mode="interp")
                output["xs" if coordinate == "X" else "ys"][interior] = smooth[trim:-trim]
                for derivative, name in [(1, velocity), (2, acceleration)]:
                    fitted = savgol_filter(values, window, order, deriv=derivative, delta=dt, mode="interp")
                    output[name][interior] = fitted[trim:-trim]
            turn = savgol_filter(np.unwrap(heading), window, order, deriv=1, delta=dt, mode="interp")
            output["turn_rate"][interior] = turn[trim:-trim]
    output["turn_rate"][~heading_valid] = np.nan
    for name, values in output.items():
        result[name] = values
    result["heading_jump"] = jump
    result["heading_valid"] = heading_valid
    axis_error = np.full(len(tracks), np.nan)
    if all(column in tracks for column in ["poseX0", "poseY0", "poseX20", "poseY20"]):
        dx = (tracks.poseX0-tracks.poseX20).to_numpy(dtype=float)
        dy = (tracks.poseY0-tracks.poseY20).to_numpy(dtype=float)
        valid_axis = np.isfinite(dx) & np.isfinite(dy) & (np.hypot(dx, dy) > 1e-8)
        axis_error[valid_axis] = ((tracks.ANGLE.to_numpy()[valid_axis] - np.arctan2(dy[valid_axis], dx[valid_axis]) + np.pi)
                                  % (2*np.pi) - np.pi)
    result["heading_axis_error"] = axis_error
    return result


def curvature(tracks, neighborhood=7):
    """Signed midpoint curvature in 1/pixel from a local cubic midline fit.

    Equal arc-spacing coordinates are centered on keypoint 10. Points 21 and 22
    (eyes) are never used. Positive sign is clockwise head-to-tail bending.
    """
    if (not isinstance(neighborhood, (int, np.integer)) or neighborhood < 5
            or neighborhood > 21 or neighborhood % 2 != 1):
        raise ValueError("Curvature neighborhood must be odd, between 5 and 21")
    indices = np.arange(10-neighborhood//2, 11+neighborhood//2)
    u = indices / 20.0 - 0.5
    design = np.column_stack([np.ones(neighborhood), u, u*u, u*u*u])
    inverse = np.linalg.pinv(design)
    x = tracks[[f"poseX{i}" for i in indices]].to_numpy(dtype=float)
    y = tracks[[f"poseY{i}" for i in indices]].to_numpy(dtype=float)
    dx, dy = x @ inverse[1], y @ inverse[1]
    ddx, ddy = 2 * (x @ inverse[2]), 2 * (y @ inverse[2])
    speed = np.hypot(dx, dy)
    valid = np.isfinite(x).all(axis=1) & np.isfinite(y).all(axis=1) & (speed > 1e-8)
    out = np.full(len(tracks), np.nan)
    out[valid] = (dx[valid]*ddy[valid] - dy[valid]*ddx[valid]) / speed[valid]**3
    return out


def collective(tracks_with_kinematics, length_scale, low_speed=.5, min_fraction=.8):
    """Per-frame heading alignment, spread and centroid-relative circulation."""
    _scale(length_scale)
    if not np.isfinite(low_speed) or low_speed <= 0 or not 0 < min_fraction <= 1:
        raise ValueError("Low speed must be positive and min_fraction in (0,1]")
    x_col, y_col = ("xs", "ys") if {"xs", "ys"}.issubset(tracks_with_kinematics.columns) else ("X", "Y")
    frames, fish, ordered, a = _dense(tracks_with_kinematics, [x_col, y_col, "ANGLE", "vx", "vy"])
    position = np.stack([a[x_col], a[y_col]], axis=2)
    velocity = np.stack([a["vx"], a["vy"]], axis=2)
    if not np.isfinite(a["ANGLE"]).all():
        raise ValueError("Nonfinite collective heading")
    relative = position - position.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
    radius = np.linalg.norm(relative, axis=2)
    centroid_v = velocity.mean(axis=1)
    all_velocity = np.isfinite(velocity).all(axis=(1, 2))
    all_geometry = np.isfinite(position).all(axis=(1, 2))
    all_valid = all_velocity & all_geometry
    relative_v = velocity - centroid_v[:, None, :]
    relative_speed = np.linalg.norm(relative_v, axis=2)
    eligible = ((radius > 1e-6*length_scale) & (relative_speed >= low_speed*length_scale)
                & all_valid[:, None])
    fractions = eligible.mean(axis=1)
    # Eligibility is unknown when the centroid-relative velocities cannot be computed.
    fractions = np.where(all_valid, fractions, np.nan)
    cross = relative[:, :, 0]*relative_v[:, :, 1] - relative[:, :, 1]*relative_v[:, :, 0]
    normalized = np.zeros_like(cross)
    np.divide(cross, radius*relative_speed, out=normalized, where=eligible)
    counts = eligible.sum(axis=1)
    circulation = np.full(len(frames), np.nan)
    signed = np.full(len(frames), np.nan)
    reference = np.full(len(frames), np.nan)
    valid = all_valid & (fractions >= min_fraction) & (counts > 0)
    signed[valid] = np.clip(normalized[valid].sum(axis=1)/counts[valid], -1, 1)
    circulation[valid] = np.abs(signed[valid])
    reference[valid] = np.sqrt(1/(np.pi*counts[valid]))
    # A little rounding can make an analytic unit circulation exceed one by epsilon.
    circulation[valid] = np.clip(circulation[valid], 0, 1)
    centroid_speed = np.linalg.norm(centroid_v, axis=1)/length_scale
    centroid_speed[~all_velocity] = np.nan
    first = ordered.groupby("frame", sort=True)[["time", "segment"]].first()
    polarization = np.hypot(np.cos(a["ANGLE"]).mean(axis=1), np.sin(a["ANGLE"]).mean(axis=1))
    if "heading_valid" in ordered:
        polarization[~ordered.heading_valid.to_numpy(dtype=bool).reshape(len(frames), len(fish)).all(axis=1)] = np.nan
    return pd.DataFrame({
        "frame": frames, "time": first.time.to_numpy(), "segment": first.segment.to_numpy(),
        "polarization": polarization,
        "spread_bl": np.sqrt((radius**2).mean(axis=1))/length_scale,
        "centroid_speed_bl_s": centroid_speed, "circulation": circulation,
        "eligible_fraction": fractions, "signed_circulation": signed,
        "iid_direction_reference": reference,
    })


def spatial(tracks, length_scale, extent=10, bins=40):
    """All-neighbor occupancy in focal forward/right coordinates.

    histogram[forward_bin, right_bin] contains raw ordered-pair counts. density
    divides by all focal observations and bin area in square body lengths.
    """
    _scale(length_scale)
    if not np.isfinite(extent) or extent <= 0 or not isinstance(bins, (int, np.integer)) or bins < 1:
        raise ValueError("Positive extent and integer bin count required")
    frames, fish, _, a = _dense(tracks, ["X", "Y", "ANGLE"])
    if not all(np.isfinite(values).all() for values in a.values()):
        raise ValueError("Nonfinite spatial inputs")
    edges = np.linspace(-extent, extent, bins+1)
    counts = np.zeros((bins, bins), dtype=float)
    nearest = np.full((len(frames), len(fish)), np.nan)
    off_diagonal = ~np.eye(len(fish), dtype=bool)
    # Chunk frame dimension so larger schools do not allocate a trial-sized NxN tensor.
    for start in range(0, len(frames), 128):
        stop = min(start+128, len(frames))
        x, y, theta = (a[column][start:stop] for column in ["X", "Y", "ANGLE"])
        dx = (x[:, None, :] - x[:, :, None])/length_scale
        dy = (y[:, None, :] - y[:, :, None])/length_scale
        distance = np.hypot(dx, dy)
        distance[:, np.arange(len(fish)), np.arange(len(fish))] = np.inf
        if len(fish) > 1:
            nearest[start:stop] = distance.min(axis=2)
        cosine, sine = np.cos(theta)[:, :, None], np.sin(theta)[:, :, None]
        forward = (dx*cosine + dy*sine)[:, off_diagonal].ravel()
        right = (-dx*sine + dy*cosine)[:, off_diagonal].ravel()
        counts += np.histogram2d(forward, right, bins=(edges, edges))[0]
    focal_count = len(frames)*len(fish)
    pairs = focal_count*(len(fish)-1)
    bin_area = (edges[1]-edges[0])**2
    return {
        "nearest": pd.DataFrame({"frame": np.repeat(frames, len(fish)), "id": np.tile(fish, len(frames)),
                                 "distance_bl": nearest.ravel()}),
        "histogram": counts, "density": counts/(focal_count*bin_area),
        "bin_edges": edges, "focal_count": focal_count,
        "shown_pair_fraction": float(counts.sum()/pairs) if pairs else float("nan"),
    }


def visibility(tracks, network):
    """Observer out-degree including zeros, restricted to covered frames."""
    _single_trial(tracks)
    if network.duplicated(["frame", "id1", "id2"]).any():
        raise ValueError("Duplicate visibility edges")
    if (network.id1 == network.id2).any():
        raise ValueError("Self visibility edges")
    track_keys = pd.MultiIndex.from_frame(tracks[["frame", "id"]])
    for column in ["id1", "id2"]:
        if not pd.MultiIndex.from_frame(network[["frame", column]]).isin(track_keys).all():
            raise ValueError("Dangling visibility endpoint")
    covered = tracks[tracks.frame.isin(network.frame.unique())].sort_values(["frame", "id"])
    keys = pd.MultiIndex.from_frame(covered[["frame", "id"]])
    counts = network.groupby(["frame", "id1"]).size().reindex(keys, fill_value=0)
    degree = covered[["frame", "id"]].reset_index(drop=True).copy()
    degree["degree"] = counts.to_numpy(dtype=int)
    per_frame = degree.groupby("frame", as_index=False).agg(mean_degree=("degree", "mean"))
    pairs = pd.MultiIndex.from_frame(network[["frame", "id1", "id2"]])
    reverse = pd.MultiIndex.from_arrays([network.frame, network.id2, network.id1])
    reciprocity = float(reverse.isin(pairs).mean()) if len(network) else float("nan")
    return {"degree": degree, "per_frame": per_frame, "reciprocal_edge_fraction": reciprocity}


def heading_diagnostics(k, length_scale, low_speed=.5):
    """Describe jumps and sustained travel opposite heading, without exclusion.

    Reverse motion can be real backward movement, a tracking error, or another
    mismatch. These diagnostics do not identify proven head-tail flips. The
    velocity eligibility denominator is computed before any heading jump mask.
    """
    _single_trial(k)
    _scale(length_scale)
    if not np.isfinite(low_speed) or low_speed <= 0:
        raise ValueError("Low-speed threshold must be finite and positive")
    angle = k.ANGLE.to_numpy(dtype=float)
    vx, vy = k.vx.to_numpy(dtype=float), k.vy.to_numpy(dtype=float)
    if not np.isfinite(angle).all():
        raise ValueError("Nonfinite heading diagnostic inputs")
    speed = np.hypot(vx, vy)/length_scale
    eligible = np.isfinite(speed) & (speed >= low_speed)
    slip = (np.arctan2(vy, vx) - angle + np.pi) % (2*np.pi) - np.pi
    reverse = eligible & (np.abs(slip) > np.pi/2)
    unmasked = k.heading_valid.to_numpy(dtype=bool) if "heading_valid" in k else np.ones(len(k), dtype=bool)
    frames = k.frame.to_numpy()
    events = []
    best = {int(fish): {"id": int(fish), "start": None, "end": None, "segment": None, "n": 0}
            for fish in np.sort(k.id.unique())}
    for (fish, segment), positions in k.groupby(["id", "segment"], sort=True).indices.items():
        positions = positions[np.argsort(frames[positions], kind="stable")]
        block_frames = frames[positions]
        step = (np.diff(angle[positions]) + np.pi) % (2*np.pi) - np.pi
        jumps = np.flatnonzero((np.diff(block_frames) == 1) & (np.abs(step) > np.pi/2))
        for index in jumps:
            events.append({"id": int(fish), "segment": int(segment),
                           "prev_frame": int(block_frames[index]), "frame": int(block_frames[index+1]),
                           "signed_wrapped_step": float(step[index])})
        # Retain only reverse samples. A false/ineligible sample or frame gap
        # breaks a run even if reverse motion resumes on the next stored row.
        indices = np.flatnonzero(reverse[positions])
        if not len(indices):
            continue
        cuts = np.flatnonzero((np.diff(indices) != 1) | (np.diff(block_frames[indices]) != 1))+1
        for run in np.split(indices, cuts):
            record = {"id": int(fish), "start": int(block_frames[run[0]]),
                      "end": int(block_frames[run[-1]]), "segment": int(segment), "n": int(len(run))}
            old = best[int(fish)]
            if record["n"] > old["n"] or (record["n"] == old["n"] and record["start"] < old["start"]):
                best[int(fish)] = record
    denominator = int(eligible.sum())
    return {
        "low_speed_bl_s": float(low_speed), "heading_events": events,
        "speed_eligible_rows_before_heading_mask": denominator,
        "reverse_motion_rows": int(reverse.sum()),
        "reverse_motion_fraction": float(reverse.sum()/denominator) if denominator else None,
        "reverse_motion_rows_outside_jump_mask": int((reverse & unmasked).sum()),
        "longest_reverse_runs_per_fish": list(best.values()),
        "interpretation": "Flags reverse travel relative to tracked heading, not proven head-tail flips. "
                          "Sustained intervals are not automatically excluded without video validation.",
        "mask_policy": "Finite speed >= threshold; heading_valid is not used in the primary denominator or runs.",
    }

The largest retained network object is one bit per possible directed fish pair
per tracked frame, padded to a byte at each frame. Row ordering is irrelevant.
A whole track frame absent from the network is unknown coverage, not zero edges.

COLUMNS = ['frame', 'id1', 'id2', 'n_rays', 'angular_size_deg', 'distance']

def _finite_numeric(values, name):
    try:
        result = np.asarray(values, dtype=np.float64)
    except (TypeError, ValueError) as exc:
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: nonnumeric values') from exc
    if not np.isfinite(result).all():
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: null or nonfinite values')
    return result


def _integer_keys(values, name):
    """Validate without losing precision on integer-typed IDs."""
    values = np.asarray(values)
    if values.dtype.kind in 'iu':
        if values.dtype.kind == 'u' and values.size and values.max() > np.iinfo(np.int64).max:
            raise ValueError(f'{name}: integer outside int64 range')
        return values.astype(np.int64, copy=False)
    numeric = _finite_numeric(values, name)
    if ((numeric != np.floor(numeric)).any() or (np.abs(numeric) >= 2**53).any()):
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: noninteger or unsafe floating-point identifier')
    return numeric.astype(np.int64)


def _dense_tracks(tracks):
    missing = {'frame', 'id', 'X', 'Y'} - set(tracks)
    if missing:
        raise ValueError(f'Tracks missing columns: {sorted(missing)}')
    if tracks.empty:
        raise ValueError('Empty tracks')
    for column in ['group', 'sequence']:
        if column in tracks and tracks[column].nunique(dropna=False) > 1:
            raise ValueError('Network summary requires one trial')
    selected = tracks[['frame', 'id', 'X', 'Y']].copy()
    selected['frame'] = _integer_keys(selected.frame.to_numpy(), 'track frame')
    selected['id'] = _integer_keys(selected.id.to_numpy(), 'track id')
    selected['X'] = _finite_numeric(selected.X.to_numpy(), 'track X')
    selected['Y'] = _finite_numeric(selected.Y.to_numpy(), 'track Y')
    if selected.duplicated(['frame', 'id']).any():
        raise ValueError('Duplicate track keys')
    frames = np.sort(selected.frame.unique())
    fish = np.sort(selected.id.unique())
    if len(selected) != len(frames) * len(fish):
        raise ValueError('Incomplete fish set')
    selected = selected.sort_values(['frame', 'id'])
    positions = selected[['X', 'Y']].to_numpy().reshape(len(frames), len(fish), 2)
    return frames, fish, positions


def _lookup(values, labels, name):
    index = np.searchsorted(labels, values)
    if (index >= len(labels)).any():
        raise ValueError(f'Dangling network {name}')
    if not np.array_equal(labels[index], values):
        raise ValueError(f'Dangling network {name}')
    return index


def summarize_network(path, tracks, output_dir=None, batch_size=131072, *, excluded_frames=None):
    """Summarize one network Parquet without materializing its edge table.

    ``tracks`` needs frame/id/X/Y for the full trial. Duplicate edges are rejected
    even across batches. Covered frames include all fish, including zero-degree
    observers. Unknown whole-frame coverage is excluded from degree denominators.
    Optional CSVs include every track frame and NaN metrics for unknown coverage.
    ``excluded_frames`` supplies track-frame IDs with any nonpositive body length
    for a separate whole-frame exclusion sensitivity; primary metrics and every
    raw-row validation retain all frames.
    """
    if not isinstance(batch_size, (int, np.integer)) or batch_size < 1:
        raise ValueError('batch_size must be a positive integer')
    frames, fish, xy = _dense_tracks(tracks)

    excluded = np.zeros(frame_count, dtype=bool)
    if excluded_frames is not None:

            raise ValueError('excluded_frames must contain one-dimensional frame IDs')
        excluded[_lookup(exclude_ids, frames, 'excluded frame')] = True

    missing = set(COLUMNS) - set(source.schema_arrow.names)
    if missing:
        raise ValueError(f'Network missing columns: {sorted(missing)}')
    float32_geometry = (any(str(tracks[column].dtype).lower() == 'float32' for column in ['X', 'Y'])
                        or source.schema_arrow.field('distance').type == pa.float32())
    distance_tolerance = 1e-3 if float32_geometry else 1e-4
    angular_tolerance = 1e-4 if source.schema_arrow.field('angular_size_deg').type == pa.float32() else 1e-9
    bits_per_frame = n * n
    bytes_per_frame = (bits_per_frame + 7) // 8
    packed = np.zeros(frame_count * bytes_per_frame, dtype=np.uint8)
    rows = 0
    angular_counts = np.zeros(len(ANGULAR_EDGES)-1, dtype=np.int64)
    underflow = overflow = 0
    max_distance_error = max_angular_error = 0.0
    angular_min = float('inf'); angular_max = -float('inf')
    distance_min = float('inf'); distance_max = -float('inf')
    ray_min = None; ray_max = None
    batches = 0
    for batch in source.iter_batches(batch_size=int(batch_size), columns=COLUMNS):

        for column in COLUMNS:

            if arrow.null_count:

            value = arrow.to_numpy(zero_copy_only=False)
            arrays[column] = (_integer_keys(value, f'network {column}')

                              else _finite_numeric(value, f'network {column}'))
        if not batch.num_rows:
            continue
        fi = _lookup(arrays['frame'], frames, 'frame')
        observer = _lookup(arrays['id1'], fish, 'observer')
        observed = _lookup(arrays['id2'], fish, 'observed fish')
        if (observer == observed).any():
            raise ValueError('Self visibility edge')
        rays = arrays['n_rays']
        if (rays <= 0).any():
            raise ValueError('Nonpositive ray count')
        angular = arrays['angular_size_deg']
        angular_error = np.abs(angular - .36*rays)
        max_angular_error = max(max_angular_error, float(angular_error.max()))
        if angular_error.max() > angular_tolerance:
            raise ValueError('Angular size inconsistent with ray count')
        distances = arrays['distance']
        actual_distance = np.linalg.norm(xy[fi, observer] - xy[fi, observed], axis=1)
        error = np.abs(actual_distance - distances)
        max_distance_error = max(max_distance_error, float(error.max()))
        if (distances < 0).any() or error.max() > distance_tolerance:
            raise ValueError('Network center distance inconsistent with tracks')
        # Validate uniqueness before OR operations; repeated byte indices are fine,
        # but the same bit means the same directed edge at the same frame.
        pair = observer * n + observed

    covered_count = int(covered.sum())
    if int(edge_counts.sum()) != rows:
        raise ValueError('Packed adjacency row-count consistency failed')
    eligible_observations = covered_count * n
    per_frame = pd.DataFrame({'frame': frames, 'covered': covered, 'edge_count': edge_counts,
                              'mean_degree': np.where(covered, edge_counts/n, np.nan)})
    reciprocity = np.full(frame_count, np.nan)

    per_frame['reciprocal_edge_fraction'] = reciprocity

def quant_check(q, name, maximum=None):
    if type(q['n']) is not int or q['n'] < 0:
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: invalid observation count')
    values = [q[k] for k in QKEYS]
    if q['n'] == 0:
        if any(v is not None for v in values):
            raise ValueError(f'{name}: nonempty quantiles with zero observations')
        return
    if any(v is None or not np.isfinite(v) for v in values) or values != sorted(values):
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: invalid/unsorted quantiles')
    if maximum is not None and (values[0] < -1e-10 or values[-1] > maximum+1e-10):
        raise ValueError(f'{name}: outside [0,{maximum}]')


def validate_trial(stem, summary, collective):
    expected_n = int(stem.split('-fish')[0])
    if expected_n == 150: expected_n = 151
    if summary['trial'] != stem.replace('__', '/') or summary['fish'] != expected_n:
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: mismatched identity or observed fish count')
    frames = summary['quality']['frames']
    required = {'frame', 'time', 'segment', 'polarization', 'circulation'}
    if not required.issubset(collective.columns) or len(collective) != frames or frames == 0:
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: collective table schema/frame count mismatch')
    for key in ['frame', 'time', 'segment']:
        if not np.isfinite(collective[key]).all():
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: nonfinite {key}')
    if collective.frame.duplicated().any() or (np.diff(collective.frame) <= 0).any() or (np.diff(collective.time) <= 0).any():
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: repeated/nonmonotone group frames or times')
    for key in METRICS:
        quant_check(summary[key], key, 1 if key in ('polarization', 'circulation') else None)
    for key in ('polarization', 'circulation'):
        a = collective[key].to_numpy(dtype=float)
        if np.isinf(a).any(): raise ValueError(f'{stem}: infinite {key}')
        a = a[np.isfinite(a)]
        if len(a) != summary[key]['n']:
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: {key} table/summary count mismatch')
        if len(a) and not np.allclose(np.quantile(a, [.05,.5,.95]), [summary[key][k] for k in QKEYS], atol=1e-10):
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: {key} table/summary quantile mismatch')
        if len(a) and (a.min() < -1e-10 or a.max() > 1+1e-10):
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: {key} outside [0,1]')
    fraction = summary['circulation']['n']/frames
    if not np.isclose(fraction, summary['circulation_eligible_frame_fraction'], atol=1e-12, rtol=0):
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: circulation coverage mismatch')
    joint = (np.isfinite(collective.polarization) & np.isfinite(collective.circulation)).mean()
    if not np.isclose(joint, summary['joint_eligible_frame_fraction'], atol=1e-12, rtol=0):
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: joint coverage mismatch')
    thresholds = summary['threshold_sensitivity']
    if sorted(t['threshold_bl_s'] for t in thresholds) != [.25,.5,1.]:
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: expected three threshold settings')
    common = summary['threshold_circulation_common_eligible']
    if not np.isclose(common['common_frames']/frames, common['common_fraction'], atol=1e-12, rtol=0):
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: common-frame coverage mismatch')
    settings = {t['threshold_bl_s']:t for t in common['settings']}
    if set(settings) != {.25,.5,1.}: raise ValueError(f'{stem}: missing common-frame setting')
    for t in thresholds:
        quant_check(t['circulation'], 'threshold circulation', 1)
        n = t['circulation']['n']
        if not np.isclose(n/frames, t['circulation_eligible_frame_fraction'], atol=1e-12, rtol=0):
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: threshold coverage mismatch')
        q = settings[t['threshold_bl_s']]['circulation']
        quant_check(q, 'common-frame circulation', 1)
        if q['n'] != common['common_frames'] or q['n'] > n:
            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: inconsistent common-frame count')
    selections = {t['threshold_frame_set_bl_s']:t for t in summary['threshold_frame_selection']['settings']}
    if set(selections) != {.25,.5,1.}: raise ValueError(f'{stem}: missing frame-selection setting')
    for t in thresholds:

        if (selected['candidate_frames'] != t['circulation']['n']
                or selected['baseline_valid_frames'] != selected['baseline_circulation']['n']

            raise ValueError(f'{stem}: inconsistent baseline frame-selection count')
    network = summary['network']
    if network.get('status') == 'not_requested':
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: network analysis is incomplete')
    if network['fish'] != expected_n or network['tracked_frame_count'] != frames:
        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: network/track coverage mismatch')
    nobs = network['observer_frame_count']

        raise ValueError(f'{stem}: network observation denominator mismatch')

    for k in ['visible_fraction_mean', 'reciprocal_edge_fraction']:

def load_trials(root, allow_partial=False):
    missing = [s for s in TRIALS if not (root/'trials'/s/'summary.json').is_file() or not (root/'trials'/s/'collective.csv').is_file()]
    if missing and not allow_partial:
        raise ValueError('Comparison incomplete; missing completed summaries/tables: '+', '.join(missing))
    records = []
    for stem in TRIALS:
        if stem in missing: continue
        directory = root/'trials'/stem
        summary = json.loads((directory/'summary.json').read_text())

def degree_quantiles(network):
    """Exact NumPy-linear quantiles from integer degree counts, without expansion."""
    hist = network['degree_histogram']
    values, counts = np.asarray(hist['degree']), np.asarray(hist['count'], dtype=np.int64)
    if len(values) != len(counts) or (counts < 0).any() or (np.diff(values) <= 0).any():
        raise ValueError('Invalid degree histogram')
    n = int(counts.sum())
    q = {'n': n}
    for key in QKEYS:
        if n == 0: q[key] = None; continue
        rank = float(key)*(n-1)
        lo, hi = int(np.floor(rank)), int(np.ceil(rank))
        cs = np.cumsum(counts)
        vlo, vhi = values[np.searchsorted(cs, [lo, hi], side='right')]
        q[key] = float((vlo+(vhi-vlo)*(rank-lo))/(network['fish']-1))
    return q

def flatten(value, prefix=''):
    if isinstance(value,dict):
        for key,child in value.items(): yield from flatten(child, f'{prefix}.{key}'.lstrip('.'))
    elif isinstance(value,list):
        for i,child in enumerate(value): yield from flatten(child, f'{prefix}.{i}')
    else: yield prefix,value

def line_with_breaks(table, column):
    """Keep actual times and insert NaNs at segment/frame gaps; retain metric NaNs."""
    times=table.time.to_numpy(dtype=float)/60
    values=table[column].to_numpy(dtype=float)
    cuts=np.flatnonzero((np.diff(table.frame.to_numpy()) != 1) | (np.diff(table.segment.to_numpy()) != 0))+1
    return np.insert(times,cuts,np.nan),np.insert(values,cuts,np.nan)

"""Run configured trials sequentially in fresh processes; never overwrite results."""
from __future__ import annotations

import argparse
import hashlib
import json
from pathlib import Path
import re
import subprocess
import sys
import time


ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[1]
INPUT_MEMBERS = ('tracks.parquet', 'networks.parquet')


def file_hash(path):
    digest = hashlib.sha256()
    with Path(path).open('rb') as stream:
        for block in iter(lambda: stream.read(1024*1024), b''):
            digest.update(block)
    return digest.hexdigest()


def read_json(path):
    try:
        value = json.loads(Path(path).read_text())
        if not isinstance(value, dict):
            raise ValueError('Expected a JSON object')
        return value
    except (OSError, ValueError) as exc:
        raise ValueError(f'Cannot read valid JSON: {path}') from exc


def downloads_ready(data):
    """The fetcher atomically saves each member before adding its provenance."""
    provenance = data/'provenance.json'
    if not provenance.is_file():
        return False
    files = read_json(provenance).get('files', {})
    if not isinstance(files, dict):
        raise ValueError(f'Invalid fetch member manifest: {provenance}')
    for name in INPUT_MEMBERS:
        path = data/name
        entry = files.get(name, {})
        if not isinstance(entry, dict):
            raise ValueError(f'Invalid fetch member entry: {name}')
        sha = entry.get('sha256')
        if not path.is_file() or not isinstance(sha, str) or not re.fullmatch(r'[0-9a-fA-F]{64}', sha):
            return False
        if entry.get('size') != path.stat().st_size:
            return False
    return True


def _resolve_recorded(path, root):
    path = Path(path)
    return path.resolve() if path.is_absolute() else (root/path).resolve()


def validate_completed(output, data, config, repo=ROOT):
    """Accept only complete results with all recorded digests still matching."""
    if not (output/'summary.json').is_file() or not (output/'provenance.json').is_file():
        raise ValueError(f'Partial output; leave untouched and choose a new output root: {output}')
    provenance = read_json(output/'provenance.json')
    manifests = {}
    for category, root in [('input_hashes', repo), ('source_hashes', repo), ('output_hashes', output)]:
        hashes = provenance.get(category)
        if not isinstance(hashes, dict) or not hashes:
            raise ValueError(f'Missing {category} in {output}/provenance.json')
        manifests[category] = {}
        for name, expected in hashes.items():
            path = _resolve_recorded(name, root)
            if category == 'output_hashes' and not path.is_relative_to(output.resolve()):
                raise ValueError(f'Output hash path escapes trial directory: {name}')
            if not path.is_file() or file_hash(path) != expected:
                raise ValueError(f'Resume refused: {category} mismatch: {path}')
            manifests[category][path] = expected
    required_inputs = {(data/name).resolve() for name in (*INPUT_MEMBERS, 'provenance.json')}
    if set(manifests['input_hashes']) != required_inputs:
        raise ValueError(f'Resume refused: input manifest does not match selected trial: {data}')
    required_sources = {config.resolve(), (repo/'src/shiners/compare_trial.py').resolve(),
                        (repo/'src/shiners/metrics.py').resolve(), (repo/'src/shiners/network_summary.py').resolve()}
    if not required_sources.issubset(manifests['source_hashes']):
        raise ValueError('Resume refused: config or analysis source missing from provenance')
    recorded_outputs = set(manifests['output_hashes'])
    actual_outputs = {p.resolve() for p in output.rglob('*') if p.is_file() and p != output/'provenance.json'}
    if (output/'summary.json').resolve() not in recorded_outputs or recorded_outputs != actual_outputs:
        raise ValueError(f'Resume refused: incomplete or unrecorded output files: {output}')
    summary = read_json(output/'summary.json')
    network = summary.get('network')
    if (summary.get('trial') != data.name.replace('__', '/')
            or not isinstance(network, dict) or 'network_rows' not in network):
        raise ValueError(f'Resume refused: wrong trial or incomplete network analysis: {output}')
    if not provenance.get('fetch_member_hashes_match'):
        raise ValueError('Resume refused: fetch member validation is absent')


def run(config, data_root, output_root, trials=None, resume=False, wait_for_downloads=False,
        poll_interval=10):
    config, data_root, output_root = [Path(p).resolve() for p in (config, data_root, output_root)]
    configured = read_json(config).get('trials')
    if (not isinstance(configured, list) or not configured
            or any(not isinstance(t, str) or not re.fullmatch(r'\d+-fish__[A-Za-z0-9_-]+', t) for t in configured)
            or len(configured) != len(set(configured))):
        raise ValueError('Config trials must be a nonempty list of unique safe trial names')
    selected = list(dict.fromkeys(trials)) if trials else configured
    if any(t not in configured for t in selected):
        raise ValueError('Every --trial must occur in config trials')
    for trial in selected:
        data, output = data_root/trial, output_root/trial
        if output.exists() and (not output.is_dir() or any(output.iterdir())):
            if not resume or not output.is_dir():
                raise ValueError(f'Nonempty output; use --resume only for verified completed results: {output}')
            validate_completed(output, data, config)
            print(f'{trial}: verified complete; skipping', flush=True)
            continue
        announced = False
        while not downloads_ready(data):
            if not wait_for_downloads:
                raise ValueError(f'Input members/provenance are incomplete: {data}; use --wait-for-downloads to wait')
            if not announced:
                print(f'{trial}: waiting for tracks and networks with completed fetch provenance', flush=True)
                announced = True
            time.sleep(poll_interval)
        print(f'{trial}: starting fresh analysis process', flush=True)
        subprocess.run([sys.executable, '-m', 'shiners.compare_trial', '--data', str(data),
                        '--output', str(output), '--config', str(config)], cwd=ROOT, check=True)


def main(argv=None):
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description=__doc__)
    parser.add_argument('--config', type=Path, default=ROOT/'configs/comparison.json')
    parser.add_argument('--data-root', type=Path, default=ROOT/'data/trials')
    parser.add_argument('--output-root', type=Path, default=ROOT/'reports/comparison/trials')
    parser.add_argument('--trial', action='append', help='Configured trial name; repeat to select several')
    parser.add_argument('--resume', action='store_true', help='Skip completed trials only after checking all saved hashes')
    parser.add_argument('--wait-for-downloads', action='store_true', help='Wait for both input members and fetch provenance')
    args = parser.parse_args(argv)
    try:
        run(args.config, args.data_root, args.output_root, args.trial, args.resume, args.wait_for_downloads)
    except (ValueError, subprocess.CalledProcessError) as exc:
